# Rocky network path — 23 October 2026

Microphone streaming is limited by the path through the TP-Link AV1300
powerline Wi-Fi extender, not by the firmware transport. No further
isolation of that path is planned.

## What failed

Rocky joined the extender's 2.4 GHz network (`The_Gup-A`). The Mac running
the brain was on the same home network. RSSI was about −35 to −49 dBm, and
`esp_wifi_get_ps()` reported `WIFI_PS_NONE`. A continuous ping from the Mac
to the router did not change the result.

Typical uplink was 7–42 microphone frames per 2 seconds. The required rate
is about 67 (one 30 ms frame). The 16-frame queue stayed full, frames were
dropped, and speaker playback sometimes underran.

## What works

The Mac uses Ethernet into the same TP-Link AV1300 and shares that
connection with Internet Sharing. Rocky joins the shared Wi-Fi directly.
In the successful test the network name was `Rocky-Test` and the brain was
`192.168.2.1`.

On that path:

- 66–67 microphone frames per 2 seconds
- no microphone drops, and the queue stayed empty
- socket-busy counts near zero
- speech playback with no underruns in the observed tests

Credentials stay in gitignored `firmware/include/secrets.h`. `BRAIN_HOST`
is the sharing gateway (`ifconfig bridge100`, often `192.168.2.1`).
`WIFI_SSID` and `WIFI_PASS` are the shared network, not the house
extender. `firmware/include/secrets.h.example` shows the shape of that
file.

## What stayed

The link task, the microphone queue, the writability check before each
uplink frame, `TCP_NODELAY`, the speaker prebuffer, and the servo pulse
path are unchanged. `LOOP_TIMING` is `0` in `firmware/platformio.ini`. Set
it to `1` to restore the 2-second loop and network lines, the microphone
counters, the slow-loop warnings, and the power-save readback. The brain
speech trace in `server/brain/tcp_probe.py` runs only when
`DESK_ROBOT_TCP_TRACE=1`.
